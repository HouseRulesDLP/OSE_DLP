These skills are common to all adventurers as there are some things you can't help but pick up as you spend your time wandering the wilderness and delving dungeons.

### Athletics
Athletics represent your capacity to perform various feats of bodily strength and coordination; such as climbing, jumping, swimming, tumbling, balancing, running long distances, running quickly, lifting heavy objects and so forth.

**Time:** Most uses of athletics only require Movement (such as jumping) or a single Action (such as a balance check).  Some activities take longer (such as swimming, running or climbing more than your normal movement).  The GM will adjudicate how long any particular task takes.

**Opposed By:** In cases of athletic competition, *Athletics* usually opposes *Athletics*.
### Perceive
Perceive is a measure of how sharp your senses are (be they the common five or additional senses granted by special abilities).  It is your general awareness of your surroundings and ability to notice things quickly (as opposed to more system actions, like [[#Search|searching]]).  For example, you would roll perception to spot someone sneaking up on you, or to listen at a door to determine what is on the other side, to determine if food smells spoiled or poisoned, and so forth.

**Time:** Most uses of perception are instantaneous, however, a character can choose to expend an Action for a closer examination.  This is grounds for a re-roll if the initial glance failed.

**Opposed By:** Most often, this skill would be opposed by [[#Sneak]], but also [[Sleight of Hand]] on occasion.

**GM Rolls:** The GM rolls Perceive checks for the player, so that the players do not know for certain if the roll is failed or if there was nothing there to begin with.

**Downtime:** Under most circumstances, if a character can closely examine an object for 10+ minutes (depending on the size/complexity of the object), any Perceive check can be assumed to succeed.  Only for cunningly hidden things (such as a false bottom to a chest), would a roll still be required.
### Persuade
Persuade represents your ability to influence the decisions or emotions of others.  This could be through reason & logic, appeals to someone's good graces, baffling rhetoric, outright intimidation or even lying.  You would roll this when doing things like attempting to bribe a guard, convince a noble to let you join them for dinner, lie to a constable about what you're *really* up to, get a potential paramour to agree to a date and so on.

**Opposed By:** Typically, this check would be opposed by *Persuade*, itself, or possibly a straight Wisdom or Charisma check (for situations like intimidation or temptation).

**Risks:** Attempts to intimidate or deceive are likely to draw negative reactions if unsuccessful.
### Search
This is your ability to conduct an systemic and organized investigation of an area or object: searching for a secret door, concealed trap, hidden weapon on someone's person, and so forth.

This can also be used as a fallback skill (at [[3d. Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]]) for performing academic research on a subject for which you do not have the appropriate [[Lore]] skill.

This is also the skilled used to conceal an object (meaning that *Search* can oppose *Search* in such circumstances).

**Time:** It takes 10 minutes to search a single object of around medium [[size]] or a 10ft x 10ft x 10ft area.

**GM Rolls:** The GM rolls Search checks for the player, so that the players do not know for certain if the roll is failed or if there is nothing there in the first place.
### Sneak
The ability to remain hidden.  A character may use this skill as part of their movement, but if they use more than 1/2 their normal movement, they make this check with [[3d. Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] (double **disadvantage** for running).  It is not possible to use this skill if being directly observed.

**Requirements:** Some degree of cover that blocks line-of-sight to the actor.

**Opposed By:** [[#Perceive]]

**GM Rolls:** The GM rolls Sneak checks for the player, so that the players do not know for certain if the roll is failed or anyone nearby is just that observant.
### Survive
This is your character's ability to survive out in the wilderness.  It includes such tasks as [[navigation]], [[hunting]], [[fishing]], [[foraging]], finding shelter, [[tracking]] and other feats of bushcraft.  For any given group travelling together, only a single character needs to make a survival check for the full party.  However, if a group splits up, then each group is required to make their own survival checks.

**Requirements:** Appropriate gear for the task (rod & tackle or a net for fishing, bow, spear or sling for hunting, etc).

**Time:** Most uses of *Survival* may be done as part of a normal day's movement (not a [[forced march]]).  If an entire day is dedicated to a task without travelling, the check may be made with [[3d. Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]].  Typically only one check is made per [[Watch]] or [[Day]] (or more often, at the GM's discretion).

**Risks:** Getting lost or coming across a hostile random encounter.
