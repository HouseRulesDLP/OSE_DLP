

### Blessing
This is your character's ability to perform the sacred rights and blessings associated with their religion.  Use of this skill is a requirement for doing things like, blessing water or an item to make it holy, concocting minor alchemical mixtures related to one's faith and other sacred creations.  This skill can only be used by those capable of invoking [[Prayers]] and [[Miracles]].

**Activities:** 
- **Create Holy Water:** Blesses one flask (roughly 6-12 ounces) of purified water, turning it into Holy Water.
- **Brew Potion:** Different religions teach the creation of specific alchemical concoctions.  
- **Sanctify Item:** Bless a weapon, armor or other item to make it temporarily holy.  This can grant the item powers based on the [[Prayers]] and [[Miracles]] the character knows.  Making such blessings permanent is a far more involved and difficulty process.

**Time:** At least one action to bless 1 flask of holy water. Potion brewing and object blessing can take variable amounts of time (possibly hours or days) depending on the exact nature and extent of the blessing.

**Risks:** Failure by half or more can result in the inadvertent creation of a [[cursed]] item.

**GM Rolls:** The GM rolls the Blessing check for the player, so that the players do not know for certain if the a blessing or curse has been created until the item is tested.

**Downtime:** The brewing of potions and sanctification often take long enough that these must be completed as downtime activities.
### Decipher
Use of this skill allows the character to understand the gist of a non-magical language or code they are unfamiliar with.  Characters who also possess the ability to invoke Prayers and Miracles may use this ability to decipher supernaturally sacred writings.  Those with the ability to cast arcane spells and likewise decipher magical writings.  This does not confer permanent understanding of the language/code in question; for that see [[Learning New Languages]].

**Time:** It typically takes at least twice as long to decipher a written document as it would take to read it if the language/cipher were already known.

**Risks:** With magical and sacred writings, it is possible to suffer a [[Magical Mishap]] or [[Curse]] if the roll is failed by 1/2 or more.

**Downtime:** A single page can be deciphered in a minute or so.  Longer documents obviously take more time, potentially requiring the use of downtime to complete the task.

### Lockpick
A successful check allows a character to open a non-magical lock without the key. [[Thieves’ tools]] are required for picking locks.  Magical locks may be attempted but will incur one or more levels of [[3d. Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] without the assistance of someone skilled in arcane magic.

**Requirements:** [[Thieves' Tools]]

**Time:** Each attempt takes at least 10 minutes.

**Risks:** Failure by 1/2 or more requires the character to pass a [[Doom Save]] to avoid breaking their lockpicks and permanently jamming the lock.
### Lore
This skill conveys knowledge of a specific subject as defined by the class or chosen when this skill is gained.  This is also the skill used to perform research into the named subject (presuming access to books, scrolls and so on).

**GM Rolls:** When performing research, the GM makes the Lore check instead of the player so that the player does not know if the information is missing or they simply failed to find it.

**Downtime:** Research is a downtime activity taking hours or days.

**Opposed By:** [[1. Common Skills#Perceive|Perceive]]

### Traps
A successful check allows the character to disarm complex, clockwork-like trap mechanisms hidden in a lock, lid, door handle, or similar. [[Thieves’ tools]] are required for disarming mechanism traps.  Magical traps may be attempted but will incur one or more levels of [[3d. Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] without the assistance of someone skilled in arcane magic.

This skill can also be used to set non-magical traps.

**Requirements:** [[Thieves' Tools]]

**Time:** Each attempt to disarm or set a trap takes at least 10 minutes.

**Risks:** If the skill check is failed by 1/2 or more, the trap may be accidentally set off.

**Downtime:** This skill may be used to construct a trap as a downtime activity, given the needed materials are at hand.
